A private household expense tool

Penny Expense Mail

Penny helps the Stewart household record purchases from bank-alert emails in its existing personal expense tracker.

How it works

Bank alerts are forwarded to a dedicated Gmail inbox. An authorized connection retrieves new messages and passes them to Penny, an assistant running in OpenClaw on a privately managed server. Penny uses an AI service to identify transaction facts, apply the household's expense rules, and update its expense records.

Expense confirmations and questions are sent to the household's existing private Telegram group.

Why Gmail access is needed

The connection requests Gmail read-only access to retrieve the forwarded bank alerts and receive mailbox-change notifications. The permission applies to the dedicated mailbox; the workflow watches its Inbox. It does not request permission to send, edit, label, archive, or delete email.

Personal use

This is a household automation, not a public signup service. This website explains the tool and its data practices; it does not collect bank details or provide access to expense records.

Read the privacy policy for details about processing, storage, and revoking access.
