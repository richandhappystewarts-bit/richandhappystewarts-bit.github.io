Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Penny Expense Mail is a privately operated household expense automation for the Stewart family. Contact: richandhappystewarts@gmail.com.

Information accessed and purpose

With the mailbox owner's authorization, the tool reads messages in a dedicated Gmail account, including sender, subject, message body, and message identifiers. Its read-only Gmail permission covers the mailbox; the configured workflow watches the Inbox for forwarded bank alerts.

The information is used to identify purchases, extract merchant, amount, currency, date, time, and spender information, check existing expense records, and maintain the household's expense tracker. Card suffixes may be used to identify the spender. The workflow is instructed to exclude account/card details and unrelated sensitive text from expense records and confirmations.

Processing and sharing

Gmail and Google Cloud Pub/Sub provide message access and change notifications. OpenClaw runs the automation on the household's privately managed server. Email content is sent to the configured AI service, currently OpenAI, to interpret transaction information; it is not processed entirely on the server. The AI provider's applicable service terms and data controls govern its handling of those requests.

Extracted expense information and clarification questions are sent to the household's private Telegram group. Expense files are maintained in the household's Obsidian vault and may be synchronized through OneDrive. These services receive the information necessary for their respective functions. Penny does not sell the data or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Original emails remain in Gmail. Expense records, OpenClaw conversation transcripts, and operational records may remain on the server and in backups. Transcripts can contain email text, even when the final expense record contains only transaction facts. There is no automatic deletion schedule currently configured; the household manages retention and deletion manually. Copies held by service providers are subject to their respective retention policies.

Access controls

The Gmail connection uses read-only OAuth authorization. Gmail refresh credentials are stored in an encrypted local keyring. Access to the server and its credential files is restricted. These measures do not mean that all transcripts, expense files, or backups are encrypted at rest.

Your choices and deletion

The mailbox owner can revoke the app's access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized Gmail access but does not automatically delete existing expense records, transcripts, or backups. Contact the operator to request deletion of those copies. Original Gmail messages are managed separately by the mailbox owner.

This public website

These information pages contain no login, bank records, tracking scripts, or forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical access information, including visitor IP addresses, as described in its privacy statement.

Updates

This policy will be updated when the tool's data practices change.
